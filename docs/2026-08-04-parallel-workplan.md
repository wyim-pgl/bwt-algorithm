## C. WP1.2 — decompose base pair precision

Partition BWTandem's called bases into three classes against adotto: inside a
benchmark region, within 500 bp of one, and further than 500 bp away. Do it per
period class and for BWTandem-P and BWTandem-F, with ULTRA and tantan as controls.

If the "extra" bases are mostly flanking (class 2), low bp precision is boundary
calibration and the fix is a trim step. If they are mostly class 3, they are whole
spurious regions and the honest framing is that the catch-all trades precision for
recall — which WP1 finding 3 already suggests, since region precision falls much
less than bp precision. Either outcome is reportable; the current draft asserts
neither.

## D. WP2.2 — maize flank autocorrelation

For the 59 curated maize arrays, take the sequence BWTandem calls beyond the curated
coordinates and measure windowed identity at the family period (180 / 358 / 156 bp)
with `src/autocorr.py`, against two controls: array interior, and random intergenic
sequence (background ≈ 0.18).

Flanks scoring near interior identity are genuine degenerate peripheral monomers and
the curated coordinates are conservative — a result, and one that feeds WP3. Flanks
at background are overcall and the fix is a boundary trim.
