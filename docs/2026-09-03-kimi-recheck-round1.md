**Findings, ranked most damaging first.**

1. **Severity: HIGH** — **Location: manuscript.md:21 and :42** — The core-hours arithmetic does not recompute. Line 42 states BWTandem "used two threads… giving 25.3, 59.6 and 33.7 core-hours" from wall times of 12.6, 29.8 and 33.7 h. ULTRA (29.8 × 2 = 59.6) and TRF (33.7 × 1 = 33.7) check out; BWTandem does not: 12.6 × 2 = **25.2**, not 25.3. Line 21 repeats "12.6 h (25.3 core-hours)". Either the wall time was 12.65 h and is being displayed rounded down, or the core-hours figure is wrong — but as printed, the one headline number a referee *can* verify fails, and it sits inside the paper's central "lower measured compute cost" claim. This looks exactly like the "re-done rounding" repair: the discrepancy was presumably noticed and the derived quantity re-derived from the rounded wall time, or vice versa. **Fix:** print the wall time to the precision actually used (e.g., 12.65 h), or report 25.2 core-hours, in both places.

2. **Severity: HIGH** — **Location: manuscript.md:38 vs :21** — A repair left its other half behind. The abstract now discloses "a widened-window tantan re-run reaches 99.2% in 12" on Arabidopsis. But line 38 still asserts flatly: "tantan defaults to a maximum period of 100 bp, which **we left at its default**, so it reports nothing above that **here**." Both cannot be true: a non-default re-run exists, on Arabidopsis at minimum. The repaired sentence names the caveat; the Introduction still reads as if it did not exist. (The re-run treatment itself is good practice — it favours the competitor — but the contradiction is a methods-accuracy defect a referee will find in one reading.) **Fix:** amend line 38 to state the scope precisely, e.g. "left at its default except for the widened-window Arabidopsis re-run reported in Section X".

3. **Severity: MEDIUM** — **Location: manuscript.md:34** — "What none of **the three 2026-adjacent designs** emits…" — but only two 2026 designs are named anywhere in the passage: longdust and AniAnn's, introduced two sentences earlier as "**Two** tools published in 2026". The third design is never identified. This is a classic editing seam: a third tool was apparently cut from the paragraph and the count was not updated. **Fix:** change "three" to "two" (or restore the third tool and its citation).

4. **Severity: MEDIUM** — **Location: manuscript.md:21 vs :42** — The same human run is described in two registers that imply different provenance. The abstract attributes 12.6 h / periods 1–2,000 bp to "a commit-`0363d8b` human run"; line 42 attributes the identical numbers to "**the regenerated** human run", and separately mentions the operating curve was "regenerated on build-matched runs". That is potentially three run generations (commit run, regenerated run, build-matched runs) with no mapping of which headline numbers come from which — and if the commit run and the regenerated run are different executions, identical wall time to three significant figures needs an explicit statement, not silence. For a paper whose credibility rests on "regenerated, not cherry-picked" numbers, this muddle is self-inflicted. **Fix:** use one term consistently and state plainly whether the regeneration reproduced the commit run's timing or replaced it.

5. **Severity: MEDIUM** — **Location: manuscript.md:34 vs :21 vs :42** — Three different human region-recall numbers for BWTandem appear with only two explained: 79.88% ("whole-genome configuration", line 21), 81.60% ("permissive setting", lines 21 and 42), and 80.53% ("catalog regions… on GRCh38", line 34, versus longdust's 41.16%). The 80.53% figure matches neither named configuration and its provenance (regenerated run? build-matched run? different GT subset?) is nowhere stated. **Fix:** one clause identifying which run/configuration produces 80.53%, or align it with the named configurations.

6. **Severity: MEDIUM** — **Location: manuscript.md:21** — The audit sentence over-concludes: "A blinded single-reader audit **supported 4 of 400** BWTandem-only calls: unmatched calls are **predominantly over-calls**." From "4 of 400 supported" alone, the only valid inference is "at most 1% were confirmed." The colon claim requires the other 396 to have been *refuted* — if any nontrivial share of verdicts was "indeterminate/unresolvable", "predominantly over-calls" is not supported. The passage does not give the outcome breakdown, so this is not checkable from this passage as stated, and the sentence as written asserts the strong version. **Fix:** state the full breakdown (e.g., "4 supported, N refuted, M indeterminate") and let the conclusion follow.

7. **Severity: MEDIUM** — **Location: manuscript.md:36** — The scoping note was inserted, but the paragraph it was inserted into still reads as if it weren't there. The setup claims T2T assemblies plus adotto "make it possible to evaluate detection tools across **the full range of repeat classes on complete genome sequences**"; the appended note then concedes the human benchmark is on GRCh38 and "the human centromeric satellite… is therefore outside this comparison." On the flagship genome the "full range" is exactly what is *not* evaluated. **Fix:** qualify the setup sentence ("across the full range of repeat classes on the Arabidopsis and maize T2T assemblies, and the adotto-defined range on GRCh38") rather than bolting a retraction onto an unmodified claim.

8. **Severity: MEDIUM** — **Location: manuscript.md:21 vs :34** — The AniAnn's boundary-offset gap is stated at two incompatible magnitudes. Abstract: "boundary offsets **two orders of magnitude coarser**". Introduction: AniAnn's offsets are "in the **tens of kilobases**" while BWTandem reports "call boundaries measured in **base pairs**". Base pairs to tens of kilobases is three to four orders of magnitude, not two. One of the two statements misstates the gap; ironically the abstract version *understates* BWTandem's advantage, suggesting a repair overshot in the cautious direction and now contradicts the intro. **Fix:** pick the measured numbers (mean/median offset for each tool) and use them in both places.

9. **Severity: LOW** — **Location: manuscript.md:19 vs :38** — The same TRF maize fact is stated in two registers: the abstract says TRF's runtime "**stayed flat**" (5.2 → 5.5 h, a ~6% rise), while line 38 says "**nearly** flat". "Flat" is the overclaim; the abstract carries no numbers to justify it. **Fix:** "remained nearly flat" in line 19.
